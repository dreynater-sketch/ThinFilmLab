# About This Project

**Who:** Andre Robert Juliao, PhD — lab leader at the Applied Superconductivity Center / NHMFL, Florida State University.

**What:** Experimental research program developing Nb3Sn thin films on copper substrates for two applications:
1. SRF (Superconducting Radio Frequency) cavities for particle accelerators
2. Resonant cavities for axion dark matter detection (ADMX-class haloscopes)

**Core process:** Hot bronze (Cu-Sn) route sputtering at 650–750°C, producing a Zone-2 Thornton columnar Nb3Sn microstructure on Cu — a capability unique to this group.

**Active work:** Characterizing surface resistance Rs(B, T) at millikelvin temperatures and multi-Tesla DC fields; pushing toward a Nature-level result demonstrating SRF-grade performance in a strong DC magnetic field environment.

**This vault:** A Claude Code–maintained research wiki built from ~500 Zotero-indexed papers. Claude Code ingests papers, maintains entity/synthesis pages, and cross-links the knowledge base. The vault is the living reference for this research program.
