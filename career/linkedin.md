## About section (long form)

> [!note] AI-drafted - review tone before posting

Dr. Andre Juliao - PhD in Experimental Condensed-Matter Physics, Florida State University (2025). I lead the Nb3Sn-on-copper thin-film effort at the Applied Superconductivity Center / National High Magnetic Field Laboratory under Lance Cooley, developing the hot-bronze route (650-750 °C) that produces Zone-2 columnar Nb3Sn on Cu for SRF cavities and multi-Tesla/millikelvin axion haloscopes.

A large part of my current role is teaching. I run two structured training tracks - a deposition track and a characterization track - that take students from reading the chamber manual to independently fabricating and characterizing superconducting films. Students move through chamber maintenance, DC magnetron sputtering of Nb and multilayer Ta/CuSn/Nb stacks, thermal evaporation of Sn, vacuum heat treatments, then Tc extraction by SQUID M(T), phase identification by XRD, microstructure and cross-section by SEM/EDS/FIB, and RF Q-factor measurements on the PPMS up to 9 T. Each track has explicit milestone checkpoints and ends with the student coating a cavity.

Collaborators: LLNL (Carosi, dilution-fridge + 9 T), University of Washington ADMX team. Published in Superconductor Science & Technology; current paper submitted on MOI-verified continuous supercurrent across a diffusion-bonded bronze seam.
