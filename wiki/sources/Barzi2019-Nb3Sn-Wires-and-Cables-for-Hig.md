## Abstract

Since the discovery of superconducting Nb$_{3}$Sn in 1954, continuous developments have led to a strong improvement of its properties. This chapter describes the evolution of Nb$_{3}$Sn fabrication techniques since the 1960s and the main properties of modern Nb$_{3}$Sn composite wires and Rutherford cables.
