# PULSED-MAGNETIC-FIELD STUDIES OF SUPERCONDUCTING TRANSITION METAL ALLOYS AT HIGH AND LOW CURRENT DENSITIES

**Berlincourt (1962)**

*Zotero collections: Uncategorized*

---

## Abstract

Data are presented that show that in concentrated alloys H/sub r/(J min gave no sig 0) (the magnetic field which first restores detectable resistance for vanishing measuring-current density) is nearly independent of defect concentration and relative orientations of field, current, and defect structure. Values of H/sub r/ (J = 10, T = 1.2 nif- K) approaching 150 kgauss were observed for certain ductile transition metal alloys. In addition, high- current-density data are presented that suggest that Ti--Nb and Ti--Ta alloys are suitable for 100 to 120 kgauss superconducting magnets. H/sub r/ (J - 10, T = 1.2 nif- K) was plotted against average number of valence electrons per atom for Ti- V, Ti--Nb, Ti-Ta, and Zr--Nb. Results seemed to establish H/sub r/ (J min gave no sig O) as a fundamental parameter approximately equivalent to H/sub c2/ of Abrikosov's theory. (M.C.G.)
