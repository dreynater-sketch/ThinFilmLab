## Abstract

An experiment is conducted to determine the effect of uni-axial strain on the superconducting and microscopic properties of bulk Nb3Sn. Methods of calculating the electron DOS are explored in order to investigate whether the strain sensitivity of the superconducting properties is correlated to changes in the electron density of states.
