# Niobium-Titanium Superconducting Wires: Nanostructures by Extrusion and Wire Drawing

**Lee and Larbalestier (2001)**

*Zotero collections: Superconducting Materials > Nb (Bulk & Film); Thin Film Deposition & Processing*

## Abstract

Nano-fabrication is not new to the manufacturers of superconducting wires, in fact the dominant commercial superconductor, Nb-Ti, relies on a mechanically induced nano-structure for its remarkable properties. In this paper we show how these wires are manufactured so that their microstructure achieves the same scale as the superconducting vortex spacing.
