## Abstract

Superconducting A15-type compounds were discovered 25 years ago. Since this early work, several thousand original publications have appeared on this growing subject. T h e present review covers the essential structural aspects together with the characteristic superconducting properties and information on electron and phonon spectra. No compilation of data has been attempted but numerous selected references guide one to detailed results where needed.
