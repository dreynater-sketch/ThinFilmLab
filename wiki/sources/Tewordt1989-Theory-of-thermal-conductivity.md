# Theory of thermal conductivity of the lattice for high-<i>Tc</i> superconductors

**Tewordt and Wölkhausen (1989)** — Solid State Communications

## Abstract

The observed peak below Tc in the thermal conductivity К of YBa2Cu3O7 can be explained fairly well by applying the BCS theory for lattice thermal conductivity limited by electron scattering. The contribution of longitudinal acoustic phonons to the electron-phonon coupling constant $\lambda$ is estimated to lie within the weak coupling range. In addition to phonon-electron scattering we have taken into account scattering of phonons by structural defects like boundaries, point defects, and sheetlike faults. More detailed information about the strength of the phonon-electron scattering and the nature of the gap (weak or strong coupling) could be obtained from measurements of the anisotropy of К in single crystals.
