# Performance boundaries in Nb3Sn superconductors

**Godeke, A. (2005)** — PhD Thesis, University of Twente | in collaboration with Applied Superconductivity Center, UW-Madison

---

## Summary

*(Partially read — 216-page PhD thesis, only preface extracted)*

Comprehensive PhD thesis by Arno Godeke on [[Nb3Sn]] performance limits for wire/magnet applications. Motivation: existing critical current density (Jc) scaling relations were empirical and inaccurate; thesis develops improved, physically motivated scaling relations for the J-B-T-ε (current-field-temperature-strain) critical surface. Focus: compositional inhomogeneities in wires and how they affect the upper critical field vs temperature (Hc2(T)) phase boundary. Work performed at University of Twente and UW-Madison Applied Superconductivity Center (Larbalestier group). PhD committee includes Larbalestier (UW-Madison), Flükiger (Geneva), ten Haken, ten Kate (Twente), Kes (Leiden).

---

## Key Topics (from preface and TOC — full read needed)

- **Critical surface**: Jc(B, T, ε) scaling relations for Nb3Sn wires — improved from empirical to physically motivated
- **Hc2 vs T**: field-temperature phase boundary in wires; effect of compositional inhomogeneities
- **Compositional inhomogeneities**: always present in diffusion-based A15 formation; how they affect critical properties
- **Transport critical current measurements**: vs field, temperature, and strain in ITER-relevant conditions
- **Performance boundaries**: what limits Jc; what can be improved; fundamental vs engineering limits
