## Abstract

• We generated a three-dimensional stress map of grains and grain boundaries in uniaxial strained Nb 3 Sn composite. • There is great inner stress difference between inner elongated grain zone and outer equiaxed grain zone. • The degree of stress concentration is quantitatively determined.
