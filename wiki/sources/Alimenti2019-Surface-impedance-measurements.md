# Surface impedance measurements on Nb$_{3}$Sn at high magnetic fields

## Abstract

Nb<inline-formula xmlns:xlink="http://www.w3.org/1999/xlink"><tex-math notation="LaTeX">$_{3}$</tex-math></inline-formula>Sn is a superconductor of great relevance for perspective radiofrequency (RF) applications. We present for the first time surface impedance (<inline-formula xmlns:xlink="http://www.w3.org/1999/xlink"><tex-math notation="LaTeX">$Z_s$</tex-math></inline-formula>) measurements at 15 GHz and low-RF field amplitude on Nb<inline-formula xmlns:xlink="http://www.w3.org/1999/xlink"><tex-math notation="LaTeX">$_{3}$</tex-math></inline-formula>Sn in high magnetic fields up to 12 T, with the aim of increasing the knowledge of Nb<inline-formula xmlns:xlink="http://www.w3.org/1999/xlink"><tex-math notation="LaTeX">$_{3}$</tex-math></inline-formula>Sn behavior in such conditions. <inline-formula xmlns:xlink="http://www.w3.org/1999/xlink"><tex-math notation="LaTeX">$Z_s$</tex-math></inline-formula>is a fundamental material parameter that directly gives useful information about the dissipative and reactive phenomena when the superconductor is subjected to high-frequency excitations. Therefore, we present an analysis of the measured <inline-formula xmlns:xlink="http://www.w3.org/1999/xlink"><tex-math notation="LaTeX">$Z_s$</tex-math></inline-formula>with the aim of extracting interesting data about pinning in Nb<inline-formula xmlns:xlink="http://www.w3.org/1999/xlink"><tex-math notation="LaTeX">$_{3}$</tex-math></inline-formula>Sn  at high frequencies. From <inline-formula xmlns:xlink="http://www.w3.org/1999/xlink"><tex-math notation="LaTeX">$Z_s$</tex-math></inline-formula>, we extract the vortex motion complex resistivity to obtain the <inline-formula xmlns:xlink="http://www.w3.org/1999/xlink"><tex-math notation="LaTeX">$r$</tex-math></inline-formula>-parameter and the depinning frequency <inline-formula xmlns:xlink="http://www.w3.org/1999/xlink"><tex-math notation="LaTeX">$\nu _p$</tex-math></inline-formula>in high magnetic fields. The comparison of the results with the literature shows that the measured <inline-formula xmlns:xlink="http://www.w3.org/1999/xlink"><tex-math notation="LaTeX">$\nu _p$</tex-math></inline-formula>on bulk Nb<inline-formula xmlns:xlink="http://www.w3.org/1999/xlink"><tex-math notation="LaTeX">$_{3}$</tex-math></inline-formula>Sn is several times greater than that of pure Nb. This demonstrates how Nb<inline-formula xmlns:xlink="http://www.w3.org/1999/xlink"><tex-math notation="LaTeX">$_{3}$</tex-math></inline-formula>Sn can be a good candidate for RF technological applications, also in high magnetic fields.
