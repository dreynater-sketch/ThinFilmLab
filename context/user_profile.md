## Research Focus

Primary: **Nb3Sn thin films on Cu substrates** via the Cu-Sn (hot bronze) route for:
- SRF cavities (accelerator applications, zero-field performance)
- Axion dark matter detector resonant cavities (multi-Tesla DC field, millikelvin temperatures)

**Unique process asset:** Hot bronze deposition at 650–750°C produces Zone-2 Thornton columnar microstructure in Nb3Sn on Cu — not reproduced by any other group on Cu without a thick Nb buffer layer.
