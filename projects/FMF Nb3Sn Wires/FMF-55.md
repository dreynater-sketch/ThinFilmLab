## Longitudinal uniformity results

- **3.2 mm:** front end shows distorted filaments; front-vs-back total area differs by ≈ **1.6 %**.
- **0.81 mm, 9-slice serial study** (10 mm intervals): total filament-region area 196,967–202,847 µm² → max difference ≈ **3.0 %**; mean filament area 3648–3756 µm².
- **Individual filaments tracked along the stack** (#2, #29, #33, #38): max area differences 4.2 / 2.9 / 5.1 / **5.5 %**.
