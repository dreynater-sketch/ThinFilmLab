Part of [[FMF Nb3Sn Wires]]. 37-filament restack of [[FMF-50-A]] stock, sitting between [[FMF-51]] (50 at.%) and [[FMF-52]] (23 at.%) in core Sn content. Parent of the [[FMF-53 Restack]] (30 × 37).

\* Asterisk meaning unstated in the deck.

## Heat treatments

210 °C/48 h + 400 °C/48 h + 525 °C/96 h + **750 °C/50 h** or **670 °C/100 h** (both applied to 0.45 mm). Longitudinal sections imaged before and after HT.

## Results

- **Tc:** onset > 18 K at both HTs.
- **H_irr (Kramer, VSM):** 22.52 T / 22.47 T — nearly HT-independent, unlike [[FMF-52]].
- SEM cross-sections at both HTs (0.45 mm); longitudinal sections show no discontinuities in cold-mounted samples.
