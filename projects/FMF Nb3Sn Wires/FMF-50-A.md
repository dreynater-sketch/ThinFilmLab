Part of [[FMF Nb3Sn Wires]]. Monofilament feedstock — Sn rod inside Cu tube inside NbTaX alloy tube. Parent stock for the 37-filament restacks [[FMF-51]] and [[FMF-53]].

## Build

Billet: Cu tube OD 0.5″ (1.27 cm), 0.032″ wall; Sn rod OD 0.436″ (1.107 cm). Mass per unit length: Sn 7.022 g, Cu 2.725 g → **58 at.% Sn / 42 at.% Cu** (calculated).

## Composition vs size (EDS, Cu+Sn core)
