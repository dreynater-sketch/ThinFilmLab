Part of [[FMF Nb3Sn Wires]]. 37-filament restack of [[FMF-50-A]] stock. The highest-Sn wire in the series (~50 at.% Sn core) and the most fully characterized alongside [[FMF-52]].

## Heat treatments

210 °C/48 h + 400 °C/48 h + 525 °C/96 h + **750 °C/50 h** or **670 °C/100 h** (both applied to 0.45 mm).

## Results

- **Tc:** onset > 18 K at both HTs.
- **H_irr (Kramer, VSM):** 22.48 T / 22.11 T (HT attribution unclear — see [[FMF Nb3Sn Wires|hub]]).
- **Transport (15 T, 4 K, 670 °C/100 h):** Ic = 55 A; non-Cu area 78,129 µm² → **Jc = 704 A/mm²** (VSM: 631 A/mm²).

## Microstructure

- **Thin A15 layer on the filament periphery at both final HTs** (outer reaction with the NbTaX tube) — absent in low-Sn [[FMF-52]].
- "Broken heart" central void in 0.45 mm filaments.
- EDS Nb profile across the A15 layer: ~19.6 → 24.2 at.% Nb (670 °C/100 h); A15 layer ≈ 7 µm in the imaged filament.
- Hot mounting alloyed Cu+Sn on the 0.45 mm wire — cold-mount values are authoritative (see hub lessons).
