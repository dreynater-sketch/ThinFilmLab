Part of [[FMF Nb3Sn Wires]]. 54-filament wire at ~32–38 at.% Sn core. **Parent monofilament stock still unstated.** Its ~32 at.% Sn is **not a deliberately-chosen intermediate value** — Manish (2026-06-16) confirmed the final-stage Sn content is set by whatever Cu tube was available ("we are tight on that"), so this wire is *not* a designed rung on a Sn ladder.

## Composition vs size (EDS, Cu+Sn core)

| OD | at.% Sn |
|----|---------|
| 13.1 mm | 37.9 |
| 7.32 mm | 37.2 |
| 4.15 / 2.3 / 1.63 mm | 36.4 / 36.1 / 35.3 (size↔value mapping ambiguous in deck) |
| 2.2 mm (hex) | 32.81 |
| 0.81 mm | 32.34 |

## Heat treatments

210 °C/48 h + 400 °C/48 h + 525 °C/96 h + **750 °C/50 h** — **quartz tube broke during this HT** of the 0.81 mm samples; two recovered samples imaged anyway.

## Results

- SEM cross-sections of both recovered 0.81 mm samples post-HT.
- Longitudinal cross-sections (cold mount): **no discontinuities** observed.
