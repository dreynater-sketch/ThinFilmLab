Part of [[FMF Nb3Sn Wires]]. Second-generation restack: 30 strands of [[FMF-53]] → **~1110 filaments**, drawn 8 → 0.88 mm. Nominal ~40 at.% Sn. The series' first test of scaling the architecture up — and where mechanical damage first appeared.

## Build / sizes

OD progression imaged: 8 mm → 5.42 mm → 4.56 mm → 0.88/0.91 mm.

## Heat treatments

- Partial: **525 °C/40 h** pull-out (quartz tube broke; sample extracted and imaged anyway).
- Full: 210 °C/48 h + 400 °C/48 h + 525 °C/96 h + **750 °C/50 h** and **670 °C/100 h**.

## Results

- **Tc:** slightly **suppressed vs parent [[FMF-53]]** at the same ~40 at.% Sn.
- **Filament breakage:** many broken filaments **concentrated near the wire periphery** at both HTs, with **leaked Sn and Cu** found in the wire — cf. [[Jewell2003-The-influence-of-Nb3Sn-strand-]] on strand geometry and filament breakage.
- **Radial Sn gradient:** A15 Sn content decreases gradually from the core outward ("same as John's experiments" = [[Tietsworth 2024]], who showed these gradients set the Tc transition spread in RIT wires).
